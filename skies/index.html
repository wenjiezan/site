Skies

This demo implements the LUT-based atmospheric scattering system described in Maxime Heckel's blog post On rendering the sky, sunsets, and planets. It calculates Rayleigh and Mie scattering, as well as Ozone absorption, using a multi-pass approach that remains performant even for complex planetary scenes.

Based on On rendering the sky, sunsets, and planets by Maxime Heckel and this paper on Martian sky rendering.
